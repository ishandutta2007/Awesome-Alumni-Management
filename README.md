# Awesome-Alumni-Management

# Top Alumni Management Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Alumni Networks, Engagement Platforms, Fundraising CRM, Mentorship & Higher-Ed Community Software*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Alumni Management**. These systems help universities, schools, and associations manage alumni data, drive engagement, run events and mentorship, support fundraising, and build lifelong community networks.

**Examples** include Graduway, Blackbaud Raiser’s Edge NXT, ToucanTech, Almabase, Vaave, Anthology Engage, Hivebrite, EverTrue, PeopleGrove, and Graduway Community (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Dedicated alumni platforms are mostly commercial. The strongest open foundation is **CiviCRM** (nonprofit/membership CRM widely used for alumni and advancement), plus niche alumni projects and general open CRMs. This section lists every significant relevant project found.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Graduway / Graduway Community](https://www.graduway.com/)**  
  Alumni engagement and community platform used by educational institutions for networking, mentorship, and lifelong connection.

- **[Blackbaud Raiser’s Edge NXT](https://www.blackbaud.com/)**  
  Leading advancement and fundraising CRM for higher education and nonprofits—constituent management, giving, and engagement.

- **[ToucanTech, Almabase, Vaave](https://www.toucantech.com/)**  
  Modern alumni platforms focused on engagement, directories, events, and community features for schools and universities.

- **[Anthology Engage, Hivebrite, EverTrue, PeopleGrove](https://www.anthology.com/)**  
  Platforms spanning campus engagement, community building, fundraising intelligence, and mentorship/career networks for alumni and students.

- **[Other commercial alumni & advancement platforms](https://www.graduway.com/)**  
  Additional solutions for alumni directories, career services, and donor engagement.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[CiviCRM](https://civicrm.org/)**  
  Leading open-source (AGPL) constituent relationship management system designed for nonprofits and associations—contacts, memberships, events, contributions, mailings, and reporting; widely adapted for alumni and advancement offices (integrates with Drupal, WordPress, Joomla, Backdrop).

- **[AlumNet & niche alumni projects](https://github.com/jungang/alumnet)**  
  Open-source alumni exhibition and management experiments (e.g. school history, AI search over alumni profiles)—useful for specialized or museum-style alumni experiences.

- **[SuiteCRM, EspoCRM & general open CRMs](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  
  Open CRM platforms that institutions sometimes customize for alumni contact and relationship tracking when a full advancement suite is not required.

- **[Drupal / WordPress alumni directory modules](https://github.com/search?q=alumni+directory+Drupal+OR+WordPress)**  
  Community modules and themes for alumni directories, class lists, and simple engagement features on popular CMS platforms.

- **[Open event & membership tools](https://github.com/search?q=membership+OR+event+management+open+source+nonprofit)**  
  Open event registration and membership systems that can support reunions and alumni chapter activities.

- **[Fundraising & donation open components](https://github.com/search?q=donation+OR+fundraising+open+source)**  
  Open donation forms and campaign tools sometimes paired with CiviCRM or custom alumni sites.

- **[Mentorship matching open prototypes](https://github.com/search?q=mentorship+platform+OR+mentor+matching+open+source)**  
  Early open projects for mentor–mentee matching that institutions can adapt for alumni–student programs.

- **[Self-hosted community platforms](https://github.com/discourse/discourse)**  
  Open forum and community software (e.g. Discourse) used by some alumni groups for discussion and engagement.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Primary open stack**: CiviCRM for contacts, membership, events, and giving—closest open equivalent to advancement CRM needs.
- **CMS directories**: Drupal/WordPress alumni modules for lighter directory and news use cases.
- **Community layer**: Discourse or similar for ongoing alumni conversation.
- **Composable stacks**: CiviCRM + CMS + email (CiviMail or external) + event tools for a full self-hosted alumni office.
- Commercial platforms still lead in modern mobile apps, mentorship UX, and integrated fundraising analytics.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**CiviCRM** is the strongest open foundation for alumni and advancement data.  
Pair it with a CMS for public directories and a community tool for engagement.  
Commercial platforms (Graduway, Blackbaud, ToucanTech, Almabase, Hivebrite, EverTrue, PeopleGrove, etc.) deliver polished alumni networks, mentorship, and fundraising workflows.  
Many institutions use Blackbaud or Graduway-class products for core advancement and open tools for secondary communities or cost-sensitive deployments. Fully open stacks work for smaller schools and associations willing to host and customize CiviCRM.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Alumni systems hold personal data subject to privacy laws (FERPA, GDPR, etc.). Ensure consent, retention, and security practices meet institutional and legal requirements.
- Open-source tools offer control and no license fees but require hosting, updates, and staff capacity. Commercial platforms shift support and product roadmap to the vendor. Choose based on data sensitivity, engagement goals, and IT resources.
